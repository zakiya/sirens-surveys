# 2026 Sirens survey: draft questions

Draft, 2026-10-31. Built from [`question_crosswalk.csv`](question_crosswalk.csv) and
the 2023–2025 results. The **Was** column gives the crosswalk ID of the earlier
question, or "new".

## Goals

1. **Build on 2025.** Keep the 2025 approach: no ISC front office rating questions,
   and a focus on the Sirens, watch parties and membership. Results aren't shared
   with the ISC.
2. **Keep what 2025 added that worked:** renewal intent, news channels, watch-party
   venues, organizations to support.
3. **Bring back the 2024 activity grid** (watch parties, tailgates, drums, Discord,
   newsletter, discounts). 2025 dropped it, and nothing else tracks how each Sirens
   activity is going.
4. **Fix answer formats that produced unusable data:**
   - free-text seating (2025: 36 spellings across 64 answers)
   - an open-ended watch-party question that's easier to answer as a checklist
   - unbalanced scales (2025 ticket rep and renewal scales had no middle option)
   - typos ("find about about", "satified")
5. **Stay short.** Every free-text question is optional.

Size: 20 questions, or 19 for people without season tickets. 7 are optional text.
2025 had 16 questions and got 65 responses.

## Intro text

> We invite our 2026 Sirens members to fill out this survey. Your feedback helps us
> plan next season and advocate for supporters with Wave FC. Results are shared with
> the Sirens Officer Group and Wave FC. **Anonymized responses, including comments,
> will also be published publicly**, with names and identifying details removed.
> Email contact@sandiegosirens.com with questions or concerns.

The bold sentence is new. Earlier intros didn't mention public release.
